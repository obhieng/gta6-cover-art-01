VICE FREEFORM Cover Studio

Turn any photo into a magazine-style Vice cover — now freeform: pick any layout, switch Freeform on, and drag every photo anywhere on the canvas, resize it from a corner, rotate it from the ring, bring it forward or send it back, with snap guides when it lines up — and drag the title block itself to any spot, tilted as you like. Every move bakes into the PNG. Behind the panels, the canvas background can be a solid colour, a 2-stop gradient (from your custom palette, one click), or a faint dots or grid pattern — and Print marks add a bleed edge, crop marks and a safe-area box to the export, so the cover is print-ready. Underneath it all: Batch mode builds 6 different covers from 6 photos at once, five procedural frames border any cover and bake into the PNG, and Smart Crop steers each panel onto its photo's focus point. The Palette Builder extracts the 5 dominant colours from your photo, you pick 3 stops, and the grade becomes a custom gradient in those colours. Pin photos to the Moodboard scrapbook with tape corners and handwritten captions, remix the cover every day with Cover of the Day, and export all four sizes on one contact sheet. Underneath it all: CRT/VHS glitch, the Darkroom tone curve, Smart Suggest, the Procedural Art Kit, speech bubbles and projects that save as JSON. Photos stay on your machine — nothing is uploaded.

Step 1 — load a photo (or use a sample), then press Next. Jump to the full editor any time: every control below stays live.

1 · Photos (up to 3)
1b · Batch mode — 6 photos, 6 different covers

Upload up to 6 photos at once and the studio builds a different cover for each — grade, layout, title and frame are picked from that photo's own pixels, so no two covers in the batch look alike. Export batch saves all six as 1080×1080 PNGs. Without uploads, Build batch uses your editor photos + scene backdrops.

2 · Looks — one click

A full cover recipe per preset: layout + grade + backdrop + title style + treatment.

2b · Template gallery — see it, then click it

Twelve finished covers as thumbnails — layout + grade + title + Photo Lab in one click. Unlike Looks, you see the result before you pick it.

2c · Series mode — 3 covers, one theme

One theme, three covers at once: the main cover (landscape) plus a square and a portrait variant, each with its own layout. Change the theme once and all three follow; “Export series” saves all three PNGs.

3 · Title engine
4 · Layout
4b · Freeform — move anything, print-ready

Switch Freeform on and the layout grid lets go: drag any panel on the preview to move it, drag a corner handle to resize it, drag the cyan ring above it to rotate it — pink snap guides appear when its centre or edge lines up with the canvas. The title block drags too, anywhere, at any angle. Z-order, print marks and the canvas background all live here, and every change bakes into the PNG.

5 · Grade
5b · Frames — border treatments, baked into the PNG

Five procedural frames for the whole cover — Film Strip with sprocket holes, Polaroid with a caption foot, Ticket Stub with perforations and ADMIT ONE, Torn Edge with a jagged paper border, Rounded with cut corners. The frame is drawn over the finished cover, so it reads on every export size.

5b · Palette — your colours, your grade

Extract the 5 dominant colours from your photo, then pick 3 stops — the grade tint becomes a gradient in your colours, baked into the PNG. Click a swatch to set the active stop (click a stop picker first to choose which one). Save up to 4 palettes of your own.

Presets — one click, editable after:

6 · Treatment

A print treatment over the graded photo — duotone, threshold or solarize.

6b · Photo Lab

Darkroom tools over the graded photo — blur softens the photo behind the title, spotlight lights the middle of each panel, color-pop keeps one hue vivid, and the film presets age the print.

6d · Darkroom — tone curve + light

A real darkroom: drag the tone curve, tune the light, shift each colour's hue. Everything renders live and exports into the PNG — the Diptych layout shows the raw photo next to the developed one.

Click the curve to add a point, drag a point to move it, double-click a point to remove it. Luma moves all channels; Red, Green and Blue move one channel.

6e · Smart Suggest — the app reads your photo

Smart Suggest samples the photo's real pixels — brightness, contrast, warmth and dominant colour — and recommends the grade, layout and title that fit it best. One click applies a suggestion.

6f · Glitch / CRT — VHS effects, baked into the PNG

CRT and VHS glitch, rendered into the pixels — chromatic RGB split, scanlines, tracking noise, displaced slices and an RGB fringe on the title. Every effect has its own switch and strength, and all of them export into the PNG. Presets set a whole recipe at once.

6g · Auto-caption — titles from your photo

The same pixel reading as Smart Suggest, turned into titles: warm night, bright gold, cool blue — three ready captions, one click applies title + tagline. Deterministic: the same photo always suggests the same three.

6c · Procedural Art Kit — drawn art, no assets

Art drawn in code over the panels — sunburst rays, palm and skyline silhouettes, a sun disc, a perspective grid floor. Each element has its own opacity, colour and scale; drag the position sliders to move it. The art exports into the PNG.

7 · Scene backdrop

A Vice City scene behind the panels — pick a blend to wash its colour over the photos.

8 · Crop, caption & ink per panel

Click a panel number, drag the photo in the preview to move it, zoom with the slider. Every panel gets its own story line — and its own comic-ink treatment.

Panel 1 of 4

Smart crop reads the selected panel's photo, finds its focus point (brightest, busiest region), and steers the crop onto it — the same photo always focuses the same place.

8b · Speech bubbles — comic dialogue

Bubbles sit on top of the cover. Edit the text, pick speech or thought, pick a style — then drag the bubble on the preview to move it anywhere.

8c · Stickers — peel-and-stick art

Small procedural stickers — star, lightning bolt, mini palm, sun, neon heart — drawn in code with a die-cut white edge, so they read on any photo. Drag any sticker on the preview to place it; up to 6 at once.

9 · Export PNG

10 · Project — save & load

Save the whole cover — layout, grade, art kit, bubbles, captions and photos — as one JSON file, and load it back any time. Your work also autosaves in this browser and comes back when you reopen the page.
