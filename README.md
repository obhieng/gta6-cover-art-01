Vice Series Cover Studio — turn any photo into a magazine-style Vice cover, three at a time. Series mode builds a main cover plus a square and a portrait variant from one theme — change the theme once and all three follow, then export the whole series as 3 PNGs in one click. The Photo Lab adds background blur, a spotlight, color-pop (one hue stays vivid, the rest drains away) and three vintage film presets (VHS 86, Polaroid 95, Chrome 99); the template gallery shows six finished covers as painted thumbnails, so you see the recipe before you click it; and a 3-step guided wizard (Foto → Gaya → Export) walks new users through, with the full editor always one click away. The v4 title engine adds a Chrome Gradient style (7 styles total, with the new Neon Chrome grade), on top of the v3 title engine — curved, 3D-extruded, gradient, sticker-outline and neon-glow titles, plus a magazine masthead bar (name + issue + date + price) and cover lines down the right edge. Six one-click Looks apply a complete cover recipe (layout + grade + backdrop + title style + treatment); the Magazine layout is a full-bleed single photo under the masthead. Underneath it all, the v2 Ink engine still turns any panel into comic-book art (posterize + edge ink + halftone dots, level adjustable per panel), with 8 layouts, 9 sunset/neon grades, 3 built-in Vice City scene backdrops with blend modes, per-panel captions, duotone/threshold/solarize treatments, light-leak + vignette, a before/after compare slider, Surprise me + 20-step undo, and one-click PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080 — or all three sizes at once. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's “Make GTA 6 Cover Art” bounty, $250 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Upload 1–3 of your own photos (they never leave your machine) or use the two bundled samples, follow the 3-step wizard or jump straight to the full editor, pick a template from the gallery or a Look, turn on Series mode to build the landscape + square + portrait set in one theme, tune the title (curved, 3D, gradient, sticker, glow, chrome), the Photo Lab (blur, spotlight, color-pop, vintage film), set the masthead and cover lines, write a caption for every panel, switch any panel to Ink for the comic-poster look, slide Before ⇄ After to check the grade, and export the cover as a PNG in three post sizes — or the whole series at once with “Export series — 3 PNGs”.
