## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080. Upload 1–3 of your own photos (they never leave your machine) or use the two bundled samples, follow the 3-step wizard or jump straight to the full editor, pick a template from the gallery or a Look, export the cover at print sizes (A4 + A3 + Poster, one click) or press “Download ZIP” for all 11 outputs as one ZIP file (4 sizes + contact sheet + 3 mockups + 3 print sizes, with a live progress bar) or “Export everything” for the same 11 as separate PNGs or stamp your own logo on it first (upload a PNG, pick a corner, size and opacity — it bakes into every PNG), save covers to the session gallery and click any saved cover to load it back, tilt the cover in 3D (sliders or one-click presets — Flat, Lean left/right, Pop-out, Showcase lean, Poster Lean, Billboard, Phone Tilt — plus soft / hard / neon-glow shadow styles) and export it as a Framed Poster, Phone Screen or Billboard mockup, upload up to 6 photos in Batch mode and press “Build batch” for six different covers at once (or “Export batch” for all six 1080×1080 PNGs), pick a frame from the Frames pack (Film Strip, Polaroid, Ticket Stub, Torn Edge, Rounded, Neon Border, VHS Frame, Comic Burst, Retro TV, Gold Foil, Blueprint) and press “Smart crop” to steer a panel onto its photo's focus point, press “Extract palette from photo” and apply the 3 stops it reads from your photo (or a preset), pin the photos to the Moodboard scrapbook, press “Cover of the Day” for today's remix, press “Analyze photo &amp; suggest” and apply the Smart Suggest card that fits, press “Suggest captions” and apply the title that fits, blend a second photo into any panel with Double Exposure (pick the blend mode and strength, or swap the two photos), develop the photo in the Darkroom (drag the tone curve, tune the light, or hit the Golden Hour preset), turn on the Glitch effects (VHS Tracking, CRT Split or Full Glitch) and add a sticker or two, try the Diptych layout to show the raw photo next to the developed cover, switch on the Procedural Art Kit (sunburst, palms, skyline, sun disc, grid floor) and add a speech bubble, tell a 4-beat story with the Storyboard layout, turn on Series mode to build the landscape + square + portrait set in one theme, tune the title (curved, 3D, gradient, sticker, glow, chrome), the Photo Lab (blur, spotlight, color-pop, vintage film), set the masthead and cover lines, write a caption for every panel, switch any panel to Ink for the comic-poster look, slide Before ⇄ After to check the grade, save the project as JSON (or let autosave keep it), and export the cover as a PNG in four post sizes — or the whole series at once with “Export series — 3 PNGs” — or all four sizes on one contact sheet.
